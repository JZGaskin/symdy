What is Symdy?

Symdy is a companion that you talk to — like a smart friend who remembers everything you've ever told them. Over time, Symdy learns what you do, what you care about, what you're working on, and what matters to you. The more you talk, the more helpful Symdy becomes.

Why people use Symdy

It remembers

Mention your kid's name once. Symdy remembers. Talk about a project at work. Symdy follows up next week. No more repeating yourself.

It learns you

Symdy figures out how you like to communicate. Direct? Thoughtful? Quick bullet points? It adapts to you — not the other way around.

It's private

Everything Symdy knows about you stays on your computer. No cloud. No accounts. No company reading your conversations.

No setup

Download. Open. Talk. That's it. No API keys. No configuration. No confusing options. Symdy just works.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants a smart partner that actually knows them. A director managing three facilities. A parent juggling schedules. Someone learning a new skill. A person with too much in their head and not enough hours in the day. Symdy remembers so you don't have to.
