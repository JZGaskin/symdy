BED — Better Every Day

What Symdy Is

Symdy is not a chat app. Symdy is an AI companion that grows with you. It remembers what matters across every conversation. It learns who you are — your work, your projects, your preferences, the things you care about. Every exchange makes Symdy a better partner.

How It Works

Remembers

Symdy stores what it learns about you — your role, your projects, your learning goals, your preferences. Nothing leaves your machine.

Learns

Every conversation strengthens Symdy's understanding. Patterns emerge. Connections form. Symdy gets BED.

Adapts

Uses your existing AI subscriptions. ChatGPT Plus? Claude Pro? DeepSeek? Symdy works with what you already have.

Runs Locally

Your data stays on your machine. No cloud. No accounts. No one else sees what Symdy knows about you.

Get Symdy

Symdy runs as an extension for pi — the AI coding harness. Install pi, then add Symdy.

# Install pi
npm install -g @earendil-works/pi-coding-agent

# Install Symdy
mkdir -p ~/.pi/agent/extensions/symdy
curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JZGaskin/symdy/main/index.ts \
  -o ~/.pi/agent/extensions/symdy/index.ts

# Run
pi

Commands

Once Symdy is installed, it works automatically — learning from every conversation. You can also use these commands:

/symdy          — See what Symdy knows about you

Symdy also gives the AI two tools — symdy_remember and symdy_recall — so your AI partner can store and retrieve context about you automatically during conversations.
